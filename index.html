Why sponsor CaseHacks?

Hackathons test the build. Case comps test the pitch. We test both.

At CaseHacks, mixed teams of business and tech students get one real problem and 22 hours. They have to build something that works and prove it's worth paying for. You see both halves of the job in one weekend.

45 of 100 points go to the problem and the business case. A slick demo alone doesn't win here.

What you get

Hire from what you saw

Talk to teams at your recruiting table while they work on your case. Then judge them on stage. You get every team's score on your track and opted-in resumes before the event.

Every team scored on the same 100-point sheet.

SAMPLE · fictional company

CaseHacks 2027 · Case brief · Page 1 of 2

Maple Ridge Credit Union: Keeping first-year members

Situation

Maple Ridge is a mid-sized Ontario credit union. Students open accounts in their first year of university, and many leave within 18 months for app-only banks. The Head of Member Growth wants a plan she can take to the board.

Data pack

12 months of anonymized account activity (5,000 members), exit survey results, and current app screens.

Limits

$250K pilot budget. Must pass compliance review. Live within 6 months.

Question

How do we keep first-year members past month 18?

  1. The situationWhat the company does, what changed, and who the team is pitching to.
  2. The dataReal or realistic data teams can analyze and build on.
  3. The limitsBudget, rules and timeline the answer has to fit.
  4. The questionOne per track. No two tracks share one.

You bring the problem. We write it, prep the data and test it. Your team spends about 90 minutes on it before the event.
